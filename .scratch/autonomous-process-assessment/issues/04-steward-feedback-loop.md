# Design independent stewardship coverage and measure corrections

Type: research
Status: open
Blocked by: 01

## Problem

The steward parks in Waiting and relies on Product Lead for ordinary activation. Useful systemic diagnoses can end at source edits without a later check of their effect under the applied definition.

## Outcome

Design an asynchronous coverage trigger independent of Product's memory: for example, an overdue assessment condition or bounded event-driven checkpoint assessment. Decide framework versus process ownership and how to avoid duplicate reviews. Define a compact correction ledger: causal class, evidence, proposed mechanism, adoption/version evidence, expected outcome, eligible subsequent cases and recurrence result.

## Acceptance

- Existing pauses, user authority and the steward's no-framework-implementation boundary remain explicit.
- No additional delivery approval gate and no recurring unchanged status notifications.
- Coverage includes long-lived coordination tasks and their current context, not only recent coding children.
- Permission failures, lost continuation, documentation gaps and acceptance errors remain distinguishable.
- Use retrospective ordinary-work evidence in preference to an artificial live trial, consistent with the user's earlier preference.
- Provide a concrete process/framework proposal for review and create implementation tickets before calling the resulting delivery complete.

## Comments

2026-11-02: Assessment supports independent coverage, not unrestricted autonomous editing of process or permissions.
